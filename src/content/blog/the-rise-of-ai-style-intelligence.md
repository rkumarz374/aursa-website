For years, fashion technology was synonymous with e-commerce recommendations.
Algorithms telling you what to buy based solely on what other consumers purchased.

But a new paradigm is shifting the focus from consumption to composition.

This is the rise of AI Style Intelligence.

## Understanding Visual Signals

Style intelligence doesn't care about what brand you bought or how much it cost.
Instead, computer vision neural networks analyze pixel compositions, measuring color harmony scores, outline ratios, texture weight, and contrast coordinates.

This data-driven framework helps isolate your style fingerprint.

Translating subconscious habits into clean, visual insights.
