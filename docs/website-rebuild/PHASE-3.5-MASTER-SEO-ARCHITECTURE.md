# AURSA WEBSITE REBUILD
## PHASE 3.5 — MASTER SEO ARCHITECTURE & KEYWORD OWNERSHIP MAP

---

## 1. SEO North Star

AURSA builds global organic search authority across two connected search ecosystems under a single unified brand entity:

1. **AURSA Retail (B2B Commercial & Category Search Ecosystem)**
   - Fashion retail intelligence
   - Fitting-room intelligence
   - Smart fitting-room technology
   - Retail personalization
   - Shopper decision intelligence

2. **AURSA Personal (B2C Consumer Search Ecosystem)**
   - AI outfit checking & feedback
   - Personal style intelligence
   - Outfit visual balance & harmony
   - Style identity & outfit confidence

**Strategic Principle**: Both ecosystems remain connected under the unified AURSA brand. AURSA does not split into separate brand domains or disjointed identities.

---

## 2. Brand SEO Model: One Mirror. Two Moments.

- **Brand Hierarchy**:
  ```
  AURSA (Brand Gateway & Parent Entity)
  ├── AURSA Retail (B2B Fitting Room / Shopper Decision Intelligence)
  └── AURSA Personal (B2C Outfit Analysis & Style Intelligence)
  ```
- **Core Umbrella Idea**: *ONE MIRROR. TWO MOMENTS.*
  - **The Retail Moment**: *"Should I buy this?"* (In-store shopper decision context)
  - **The Personal Moment**: *"Should I wear this?"* (Daily outfit decision context)
  - **The Shared Core Question**: *"Does this actually work for me?"*
  - **Brand Promise**: *"Wear with Confidence."*
- **SEO Alignment**: Retail and Personal pages target distinct search intent queries, but anchor back to the unified AURSA identity.

---

## 3. Global SEO Language Strategy

To capture global search volume while remaining relevant in regional markets (e.g., India and South Asia), AURSA adopts a dual terminology framework:

- **Global / International Priority**:
  - `fitting room`
  - `smart fitting room`
  - `fitting room intelligence`
  - `fitting room analytics`
- **India / Regional Natural Inclusion**:
  - `trial room`
  - `smart trial room`
  - `trial room technology`
  - `intelligent trial room`

**Rule**: Do NOT create separate duplicate landing pages for "fitting room" vs "trial room". Incorporate both naturally on dedicated category and pillar pages to capture regional intent without creating keyword cannibalization or doorway pages.
